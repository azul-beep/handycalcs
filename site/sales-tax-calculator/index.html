Formulas

  • Add tax: total = price × (1 + rate ÷ 100). $100 at 7.25% = $107.25.
  • Remove tax: price = total ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). $107.25 at 7.25% = $100.

Sales tax in the US

There is no national sales tax. Each state sets a base rate and many counties and cities add their own, so the combined rate can go from 0% (Oregon, Montana, New Hampshire, Delaware) to more than 10% in some cities. Groceries are taxed at a lower rate or not at all in many states. Check your receipt or your state's revenue department for the exact rate.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate sales tax backwards?

Divide the total by 1 plus the rate as a decimal. With 8%: total ÷ 1.08.

Which states have no sales tax?

Alaska (state level), Delaware, Montana, New Hampshire and Oregon.

Is sales tax charged on tips?

Generally no, a voluntary tip is not taxed; mandatory service charges often are.
