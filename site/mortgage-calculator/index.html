How the monthly payment is calculated

The loan part uses the standard amortization formula: M = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where P is the loan amount, r the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12) and n the number of payments. Then the calculator adds one twelfth of the yearly property tax and insurance, PMI when the down payment is under 20%, and HOA fees. That full number is often called PITI.

Example

A $400,000 home with 20% down is a $320,000 loan. At 6.5% for 30 years, principal and interest are about $2,023 a month. Add $367 of property tax (1.1% a year) and $150 of insurance and the payment is about $2,540.

What is PMI?

Private mortgage insurance protects the lender on conventional loans with less than 20% down. It usually costs 0.3% to 1.5% of the loan per year. You can ask to cancel it when you reach 20% equity, and it ends automatically at 22% equity (78% of the original value). FHA loans have their own mortgage insurance (MIP) with different rules.

How much house can I afford?

A common rule is to keep the full housing payment under 28% of your gross monthly income and all debts under 36%. Lenders may approve more, but a payment that fits your budget leaves room for repairs and savings. Try the family budget calculator to check.

15 or 30 years?

A 15-year loan has a higher payment but usually a lower rate and much less total interest. Switch the term above and compare the "Total interest" line.

Frequently asked questions

Does this mortgage calculator include taxes and insurance?

Yes. Enter your yearly property tax rate and insurance premium and they are added to the monthly payment, along with PMI and HOA.

What is a typical property tax rate?

The US average is around 1% of the home value per year, but it goes from about 0.3% to over 2% depending on the state and county. Check your county assessor's site.

How can I avoid PMI?

Put 20% down, use a piggyback loan, or look at loan programs without PMI (such as VA loans). PMI can be removed later once you have 20% equity.
