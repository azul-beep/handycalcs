How overtime is calculated

Under the federal Fair Labor Standards Act, non-exempt employees earn at least 1.5 times their regular rate for every hour over 40 in a workweek. At $18 an hour, overtime is $27 an hour. With 40 regular hours and 6 overtime hours the week pays $720 + $162 = $882.

Daily overtime and double time

A few states, like California, also require overtime after 8 hours in a day and double time after 12 hours. Most states, including Utah and Texas, follow only the federal 40-hour weekly rule.

Tipped employees

For tipped workers, overtime is based on the full minimum wage, not the reduced tipped wage. Check your pay stub if the overtime rate looks low.

Frequently asked questions

What is time and a half for $20 an hour?

$30 an hour.

Is overtime calculated per day or per week?

Federal law uses the workweek (over 40 hours). Some states add daily overtime.

Do part-time workers get overtime?

Yes, if they work more than 40 hours in a week and are non-exempt.
