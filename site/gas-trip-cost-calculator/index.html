The formula

Fuel cost = miles ÷ MPG × price per gallon. A 240-mile trip each way (480 miles) in a car that gets 28 MPG uses about 17.1 gallons. At $3.29 a gallon that is $56.40, or $14.10 each for four people.

Tips to spend less on gas

  • Keep tires properly inflated; low pressure can cost 1–3% in fuel economy.
  • Drive at 60–65 mph instead of 75–80 on the highway.
  • Remove roof racks you are not using.
  • Compare gas prices along the route with a gas price app.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find my car's MPG?

Check the window sticker or fueleconomy.gov, or divide the miles driven by the gallons used at your next fill-up.

Does this include tolls and parking?

No, only fuel. Add tolls and parking separately.

Can I use kilometers?

Convert kilometers to miles first: 1 km = 0.621 miles.
