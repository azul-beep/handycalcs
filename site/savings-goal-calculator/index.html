How it works

Each month the calculator adds your deposit and the interest earned on the balance, until the balance reaches the goal. With $1,500 saved, $400 a month and 4% interest, a $10,000 goal takes about 21 months.

Ways to get there faster

  1. Automate the deposit on payday so it happens before you spend.
  2. Use a high-yield savings account; online banks often pay several times more than big banks.
  3. Send raises, tax refunds and side income straight to the goal.

How big should an emergency fund be?

Three to six months of essential expenses. Use the family budget calculator to know that number.

Frequently asked questions

How much should I save each month?

A common target is 20% of take-home pay (see the 50/30/20 calculator), but any automatic amount is a good start.

Is the interest guaranteed?

No. Savings account rates change. The result is an estimate.

Does it include taxes on interest?

No. Interest is taxable income in the US, but the effect on the timeline is small.
