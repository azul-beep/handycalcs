How age is calculated

The calculator counts full years since the date of birth, then full months, then the remaining days, the same way people say "34 years, 5 months and 2 days". It handles leap years and months with different numbers of days.

Uses

  • Checking the exact age on a form, an application or a contract date.
  • Knowing how old a child will be on the first day of school or a sports cut-off date.
  • Counting days lived, or the days left to the next birthday.

Born on February 29?

In non-leap years most people celebrate on February 28 or March 1. Legally, many US states consider that you turn a year older on March 1 in a non-leap year. This calculator counts the next birthday from February 28 of those years.

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate my age in days?

Enter your date of birth and the calculator shows the total days lived, counting leap years.

Can I find someone's age on a past or future date?

Yes. Change the second date to any date, for example a school cut-off or a retirement date.

Is my birth date saved?

No. Everything is calculated on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.
