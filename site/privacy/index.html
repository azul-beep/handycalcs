Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15. This policy explains what data Handy Calcs (https://gethandycalcs.pages.dev/), operated by Skye Solutions AI, collects and why.

Numbers you type

All calculations happen on your device. The values you enter are not sent to us or stored.

Analytics

If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which calculators are used. Google may collect your IP address (shortened), device and browser information, and pages visited. See Google's privacy policy.

Advertising

We show ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info. If you choose "Necessary only", we ask Google to show non-personalized ads. Learn how Google uses data from partner sites.

Meta Pixel

If you accept cookies, we use the Meta Pixel to measure visits that come from Facebook or Instagram ads. See Meta's privacy policy.

Affiliate links

Some pages contain affiliate links. If you buy through them we may earn a commission. The partner may set its own cookies when you click.

Your choices and rights

You can change your cookie choice at any time with "Cookie settings" at the bottom of every page. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws can ask what data is collected about them and request deletion; because we do not store personal data ourselves, requests about analytics and ads are handled by Google and Meta, and we will help you with them. We do not sell personal information. We honor Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of personalized ads.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
