The formula

Hourly rate = yearly salary ÷ (hours per week × weeks per year). A $50,000 salary at 40 hours a week is $50,000 ÷ 2,080 = $24.04 per hour.

Compare offers with real hours

Salaried jobs often mean more than 40 hours. If a $55,000 job really takes 50 hours a week, it pays $21.15 an hour, less than a $23 hourly job at 40 hours with overtime pay. Put the real hours in the calculator to see it.

Do not forget benefits

Health insurance, retirement match and paid time off can be worth thousands of dollars a year. Add their value to the salary when comparing.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $50,000 a year per hour?

About $24.04 an hour at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.

How much is $60,000 a year per hour?

About $28.85 an hour full time.

Do salaried employees get overtime?

Only non-exempt employees. Many salaried roles are exempt and do not earn overtime.
