Why cups to grams depends on the ingredient

A cup measures volume, a gram measures weight. A cup of flour weighs about 125 g, while a cup of sugar weighs about 200 g and a cup of honey about 340 g. That is why you need to choose the ingredient when converting between volume and weight.

Tips

Spoon flour into the cup and level it; scooping straight from the bag packs it and can add 20% more. For baking, a cheap kitchen scale is the single best upgrade for consistent results.

Frequently asked questions

How many grams is 1 cup of flour?

About 120–125 g for all-purpose flour spooned and leveled.

How many tablespoons are in a cup?

16 tablespoons, or 48 teaspoons.

Is a US cup the same as a metric cup?

No. A US cup is about 237 ml; a metric cup (Australia, Canada recipes) is 250 ml.
