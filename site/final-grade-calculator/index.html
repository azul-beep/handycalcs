The formula

Needed = (goal − current × (1 − weight)) ÷ weight, with the weight as a decimal. With an 85% now, a 90% goal and a final worth 25%: (90 − 85 × 0.75) ÷ 0.25 = 105%. Without extra credit that A is out of reach, but an 80% (B−) is easy: you need 65%.

Where to find the weight of the final

Look at the syllabus: it lists how much homework, quizzes, tests and the final count. If your "current grade" in the online gradebook already includes everything except the final, use it as is.

Letter grade cut-offs

The most common scale in the US is A = 90–100, B = 80–89, C = 70–79, D = 60–69 and F = below 60, but some schools use a 7-point scale or plus and minus grades. Use the cut-off your teacher uses as your goal.

Frequently asked questions

What if I need more than 100%?

The goal is not reachable with the final alone. Ask your teacher about extra credit or aim for the next grade down.

How much will the final change my grade?

The 'if you get 0' and 'if you get 100' lines show the lowest and highest grades you can end with.

Does this work for weighted categories?

Yes, as long as your current grade is the weighted grade for everything except the final.
