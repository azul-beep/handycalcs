How to scale a recipe

Divide the servings you want by the servings in the recipe. That number is the scaling factor. Going from 4 to 10 servings is a factor of 2.5, so 2 cups of flour become 5 cups and 3/4 cup of milk becomes 1 7/8 cups.

Tips for good results

  • Baking: weigh flour and sugar when you scale more than 2×; cups get less accurate.
  • Eggs: round to the nearest whole egg, or beat one and use part of it.
  • Salt and spices: start with a little less than the scaled amount and adjust to taste.
  • Pans and time: a bigger batch may need a bigger pan or two pans, and the baking time changes.

Supported formats

Whole numbers (2), decimals (1.5), fractions (3/4), mixed numbers (1 1/2) and symbols (½). Lines without a number at the start stay as they are.

Frequently asked questions

How do I halve a recipe?

Set the servings you want to half the original (for example 4 → 2). The factor will be 0.5.

Can I convert cups to grams too?

Yes, use the cooking measurement converter for each ingredient.

Does baking time change when I double a recipe?

Usually yes. Check for doneness earlier and later than the recipe says, or bake in two pans.
