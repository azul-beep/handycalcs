How to make a family budget in 4 steps

  1. Income: add every take-home paycheck in the house for a month.
  2. Fixed expenses: rent, insurance, car payment, phone. Look at last month's bank statement.
  3. Variable expenses: groceries, gas, eating out. Average the last three months.
  4. Compare: if the leftover is negative, cut wants first; if it is positive, give it a job (emergency fund, debt, savings goal).

Healthy guidelines

  • Housing at or under 30% of take-home income.
  • Emergency fund of 3 to 6 months of expenses.
  • Total debt payments (excluding mortgage) under 15%.

Do not forget irregular costs

Car registration, birthdays, holidays and back-to-school show up every year. Divide their yearly total by 12 and add it to "Everything else".

Frequently asked questions

How much should a family spend on groceries?

It depends on size and location; a family of four in the US often spends $900–$1,300 a month on a moderate plan.

What percentage of income should go to rent?

A common guideline is 30% of take-home pay or less.

Is my data saved?
