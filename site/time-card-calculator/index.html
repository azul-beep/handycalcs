How to use it

For each day, enter the time you started and the time you finished, and the minutes of unpaid break (for example a 30-minute lunch). Leave a day empty if you did not work. Shifts that end after midnight are handled automatically.

Hours and minutes vs. decimal hours

Payroll uses decimal hours: 7 hours 45 minutes is 7.75 hours, not 7.45. To convert, divide the minutes by 60 (45 ÷ 60 = 0.75). The calculator shows both.

Overtime

Under federal law (FLSA), non-exempt employees get at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. Some states, like California, also require overtime after 8 hours in a day. This calculator uses the weekly 40-hour rule; check the overtime calculator for other cases.

Example

Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 17:30 with a 30-minute lunch is 8 hours a day, 40 hours a week. At $18 an hour that is $720 before taxes.

Frequently asked questions

Are breaks paid?

Under federal rules, short breaks of 5 to 20 minutes are paid, while meal breaks of 30 minutes or more where you are fully relieved can be unpaid. Enter only the unpaid minutes.

How do I calculate hours worked between two times?

Subtract the start time from the end time and then the unpaid break. 8:30 to 17:15 is 8 h 45 m; minus 30 minutes of lunch is 8 h 15 m, or 8.25 hours.

Does it work for night shifts?

Yes. If the end time is earlier than the start time, the calculator assumes the shift ended the next day.
