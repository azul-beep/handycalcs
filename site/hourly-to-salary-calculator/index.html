The formula

Yearly pay = hourly wage × hours per week × weeks per year. Full time is usually 40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours. At $18 an hour that is $37,440 a year, $3,120 a month and $1,440 every two weeks.

Unpaid time off

If you do not get paid vacation, lower the weeks per year. Two unpaid weeks means 50 weeks. Tipped workers can add their average tips per hour to the wage to see their real income.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $20 an hour per year?

$41,600 before taxes at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.

Is this before or after taxes?

Before taxes (gross pay). Take-home pay depends on federal, state and payroll taxes.

How many work hours are in a year?

2,080 for a 40-hour week with 52 weeks.
