Frequently asked questions

Are these calendars really free?

Yes. Download and print them as many times as you like for personal, classroom or office use. No sign-up or email required.

What paper size should I use?

Every calendar comes in US Letter (8.5 x 11 in) and A4. Pick the button that matches the paper in your printer.

How do I print them so they look right?

Open the PDF and choose "Actual size" or 100% scale in the print dialog. If your printer cuts off the edges, use "Fit to printable area".

Which holidays are included?

The English calendars mark all US federal holidays for 2027, including the observed dates when a holiday falls on a weekend.

Does the week start on Sunday or Monday?

The English version starts on Sunday. The Spanish version starts on Monday.
