About Handy Calcs

Handy Calcs is a small collection of free, fast calculators for the money questions people have every day: how much to tip, how to split a check, what a dish should cost, what an hourly wage means per year, and how to make a household budget work.

Every calculator runs in your browser. We do not ask for your name or email and we do not store the numbers you type.

The site is made by Skye Solutions AI LLC, a small business in Utah, USA, with experience in restaurants and hospitality. It is available in English and Spanish.
