How to convert an hourly wage to a yearly salary

Comparing a job that pays $22 an hour with one that pays $48,000 a year? You need both in the same unit. The math is simple, once you know the standard number of work hours in a year.

The quick formula

Yearly salary = hourly wage × hours per week × 52

A full-time job is usually 40 hours a week, and 40 × 52 = 2,080 hours a year. So:

  • $15 an hour × 2,080 = $31,200 a year
  • $20 an hour × 2,080 = $41,600 a year
  • $25 an hour × 2,080 = $52,000 a year
  • $30 an hour × 2,080 = $62,400 a year

Shortcut: double the hourly wage and add three zeros for a rough yearly figure. $20 an hour is about $40,000 a year.

From salary to hourly

Hourly = yearly salary ÷ 2,080. A $48,000 salary is about $23.08 an hour, so it pays a bit more than a $22 an hour job at 40 hours a week.

What changes the real number

  • Part-time hours: use your real weekly hours instead of 40. $18 an hour at 25 hours a week is $23,400 a year.
  • Unpaid time off: if you are not paid for holidays or vacation, subtract those weeks. Two unpaid weeks means 50 weeks instead of 52.
  • Overtime: hourly workers who are not exempt usually get 1.5 times their rate for hours over 40 in a week, which can add a lot. Salaried exempt employees usually do not.
  • Tips and bonuses: add them separately, using a realistic monthly average.
  • Benefits: health insurance, retirement matching and paid time off are worth real money. Two offers with the same pay can be very different.
