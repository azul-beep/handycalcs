How the split works

The calculator adds the tax and the tip to the subtotal and divides the total by the number of people. Tip is calculated on the pre-tax subtotal by default, which is what most etiquette guides recommend. Switch it to "No" to tip on the total including tax.

Example

Four friends, a $120 subtotal, $8.40 of tax and a 20% tip. The tip is $24, the total is $152.40, and each person pays $38.10. Rounded up, each pays $39 and the server gets an extra $3.60.

When an even split is not fair

If someone only had a salad and someone else had steak and two drinks, it is fairer to pay for what each person ordered and add the same tip percentage to each. Ask the server for separate checks before ordering when the group is big; many restaurants cannot split after the bill is printed.

Frequently asked questions

How do you split a bill with tax and tip?

Add tax and tip to the subtotal, then divide by the number of people. This calculator does it in one step.

Should tip be calculated before tax?

Most etiquette experts say yes. The default here tips on the pre-tax subtotal; you can change it.

What if someone pays in cash?

Use the rounded-up amount so the total covers the bill. The leftover goes to the tip.
