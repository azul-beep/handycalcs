Image Converter

Turn WebP images into JPG or PNG, make PNGs lighter as JPG, or create WebP for your website. No upload needed.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Choose the format you want: JPG, PNG or WebP.
  2. Choose or drop the images (up to 30).
  3. Download each converted file.

Which format should I use?

  • JPG: photos. Light and accepted everywhere.
  • PNG: logos, screenshots and images with a transparent background.
  • WebP: websites. Usually 25-35% lighter than JPG with the same look.

When you convert a transparent PNG to JPG, the transparent parts become white, because JPG has no transparency.

Frequently asked questions

How do I open a WebP image I downloaded?

Convert it here to JPG or PNG and it opens in any program.

Can I convert HEIC photos from my iPhone?

Only if your browser can open HEIC (Safari on Mac and iPhone can). On other browsers, change the iPhone camera to 'Most Compatible' in Settings > Camera > Formats.

Are my images uploaded?

No. The conversion happens on your device.
