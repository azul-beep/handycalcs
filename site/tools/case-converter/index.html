Case Converter

Paste text and switch it between upper case, lower case, title case and sentence case in one click.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Paste your text in the left box.
  2. Tap the style you want. The result appears on the right.
  3. Tap Copy result and paste it wherever you need.

When it helps

  • Text typed with Caps Lock on by mistake.
  • Headlines and titles in Title Case (small words like "and", "of" and "the" stay lower case).
  • Spreadsheets with names in all capitals.
  • Sentence case for emails and descriptions.

Accented letters such as á, é, ñ and ü are converted correctly.

Frequently asked questions

What is Title Case?

Each important word starts with a capital letter: 'The Best Tacos in Salt Lake City'.

What is Sentence case?

Only the first letter of each sentence is a capital, like normal writing. Names will need a manual capital.

Is my text saved?

No. Everything happens in your browser.
