QR Code Generator

Paste a link or text and your QR code appears right away. Download it as PNG or SVG and print it anywhere.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Paste your link (for example your menu, website, Google review page or Instagram) or any text.
  2. Pick the size and colors. Keep the code dark on a light background so every phone can read it.
  3. Download PNG for screens and documents, or SVG for print. Test it with your phone camera before printing.

Free forever, and it never stops working

Many QR sites give you a "dynamic" code that points to their own server and stops working when the free trial ends. This tool makes a static QR code: your link is written inside the code itself, so it works forever and nobody can switch it off. The only catch: if the link changes, you make a new code (it takes one second).

Ideas for small businesses

  • Restaurant menu on every table.
  • "Leave us a review" sign at the register (paste your Google review link).
  • Instagram or TikTok profile on flyers and business cards.
  • Payment link (Venmo, PayPal, Cash App) next to the tip jar.

Frequently asked questions

Do these QR codes expire?

No. They are static codes with your link written inside, so they keep working as long as the link works.

Can I use the QR code commercially?

Yes. The codes you make are yours to use on menus, flyers, packaging and ads, with no attribution needed.

Why won't my QR code scan?

Usually low contrast (light code on light background), a code printed too small (keep it at least 2 cm or 0.8 in wide) or a very long link. Use dark on light and shorten long links.
