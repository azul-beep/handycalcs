Random Number Generator

Choose a range, how many numbers you need and whether they can repeat. Perfect for raffles with numbered tickets.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Set the minimum and maximum (for example 1 and 100).
  2. Choose how many numbers you need and tick 'No repeats' for raffles.
  3. Tap Generate.

The numbers come from your browser's cryptographic random generator, so every number in the range has the same chance. For a raffle with numbered tickets, set the range to the first and last ticket, choose the number of prizes and keep 'No repeats' on.

Frequently asked questions

Can I generate negative numbers?

Yes. Use a negative minimum, for example -50 to 50.

What is the maximum amount of numbers?

Up to 1,000 at once.

Is it truly random?

It uses crypto.getRandomValues, the same secure generator used for passwords and encryption.
