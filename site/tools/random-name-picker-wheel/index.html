Random Name Picker Wheel

Write one name per line and spin. The result is truly random and fair, and you can remove each winner after the spin.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste the names, one per line (up to 200).
  2. Tap Spin the wheel, or tap the wheel itself.
  3. Turn on 'Remove the winner' to pick several people in a row without repeats.

Is it really random?

Yes. The winner is chosen with your browser's cryptographic random generator (the same kind used for passwords) before the wheel starts turning; the animation just shows the result. Every name has exactly the same chance.

Ideas

  • Teachers: pick a student to answer, a helper of the day or the order of presentations.
  • Raffles and giveaways at events or live streams.
  • Family: who washes the dishes, which movie to watch, where to eat.

Frequently asked questions

How many names can I add?

Up to 200. With many names the labels get small, but the pick is still fair.

Are the names saved?

No. They stay only on your screen.

Can I use it in class on a projector?

Yes. It works in any browser, on a phone, tablet or classroom computer.
