Word Counter

Paste your essay, post or cover letter and see the word and character count update as you type.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Type or paste your text in the box.
  2. Words, characters, sentences, paragraphs and reading time update instantly.
  3. Check the most used words to avoid repeating yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How is reading time calculated?

We use 230 words per minute, the average adult silent reading speed, and 140 words per minute for speaking out loud.

Do numbers and hyphenated words count?

Yes. Numbers count as words, and a hyphenated word like 'well-known' counts as one word, the same way Microsoft Word and Google Docs count it.

Is my text saved?

No. Nothing is stored or sent. If you reload the page, the box is empty again.
