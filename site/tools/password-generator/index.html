Password Generator

Get strong, random passwords in one tap. They are created on your device and never sent or stored.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Choose the length. 16 characters or more is a good default.
  2. Choose the types of characters. Some sites do not accept symbols; untick them if needed.
  3. Copy the password and save it in your password manager.

What makes a password strong

Length matters more than anything. A random 16-character password with letters, numbers and symbols has more than 100 bits of entropy: it would take far longer than a lifetime to guess. Never reuse a password on two sites; a password manager remembers them for you.

The passwords are generated with crypto.getRandomValues, the secure random generator built into your browser, and every password includes at least one of each type you choose.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to create passwords on a website?

This page creates them inside your browser and never sends them anywhere. You can even load the page, turn off your Wi-Fi and it still works.

How long should my password be?

At least 12 characters, and 16 or more for email, banking and your password manager.

What does 'avoid look-alike characters' do?

It removes I, l, 1, O, 0 and o, so the password is easier to read and type by hand, for example a Wi-Fi password.
