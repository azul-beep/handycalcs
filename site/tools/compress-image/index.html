Compress Image

Shrink photos for email, job applications, government forms and websites. Set a size limit like 100 KB and the tool does the rest.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Choose a quality (75% looks the same to the eye and is much lighter) or type a maximum size in KB.
  2. Optional: set a maximum width. 1920 px is plenty for screens; 1200 px for websites.
  3. Choose or drop your images. Each one shows the new size and a Download button.

Your photos stay on your device

Most compressors upload your photos to a server. This one works inside your browser: the images are never sent anywhere, which matters for ID cards, documents and family photos.

Compress to an exact size (100 KB, 50 KB, 20 KB)

Forms for visas, school portals and job sites often say "maximum 100 KB" or "under 50 KB". Type that number in Max size in KB and the tool tries different quality levels until the photo fits, and makes it a little smaller if it still does not fit.

Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to compress private photos here?

Yes. Nothing is uploaded. The compression runs on your phone or computer, and the result is saved straight to your downloads.

Why is my PNG still big?

PNG is a lossless format, so quality does not apply. Choose 'Save as JPG' or WebP to make photos much smaller. Keep PNG only for logos and images with transparency.

Does compressing reduce quality?

A little, but at 70-80% the difference is very hard to see while the file is often 60-80% smaller.
