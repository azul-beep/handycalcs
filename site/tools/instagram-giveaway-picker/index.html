Giveaway Comment Picker

Copy the comments from your post, paste them here and pick one or more winners. No need to connect your Instagram account.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Open your post on a computer, select all the comments and copy them (or export them with any tool). Paste them here, one comment per line.
  2. Set the rules: number of winners, alternates, minimum tagged friends and accounts to exclude (like your own).
  3. Tap Pick the winner. Take a screenshot of the result, with the date and time, to share in your stories.

A fair draw your followers can trust

The tool removes duplicate entries from the same account (if you turn that on), checks the tagged friends rule and chooses the winners with your browser's cryptographic random generator. The date and time are shown with the result so you can post proof.

We never ask for your Instagram password and nothing is sent anywhere. The usernames stay on your screen.

Frequently asked questions

How does it read the username?

If a line has an @username, the first one is taken as the person who commented. If not, the first word of the line is used.

Can someone win twice?

Not when 'One entry per person' is on. Turn it off if your rules give one entry per comment.

Does it work for TikTok and Facebook?

Yes. It works with any list of names or comments, from any app or even a spreadsheet.
