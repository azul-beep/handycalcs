Online Timer and Stopwatch

Set a countdown with an alarm, or use the stopwatch. Tap full screen to show it on a projector or TV.

05:00

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Tap a preset (5 min, 25 min…) to start right away, or type hours, minutes and seconds and tap Start.
  2. When time is up the screen blinks and an alarm sounds three times.
  3. Switch to Stopwatch to measure time instead.

Ideas

  • Classroom activities and tests, on the projector in full screen.
  • Pomodoro: 25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes of break.
  • Cooking, workouts and rest between climbing attempts.
  • Meetings and presentations with a time limit.

Keep this tab open. Some phones pause timers in background tabs to save battery.

Frequently asked questions

Why did I not hear the alarm?

Check the volume and that the phone is not on silent. Browsers only allow sound after you tap the page once, which happens when you press Start.

Does it keep counting if I switch tabs?

Yes, it uses the clock time, so it stays exact. The alarm sounds when you come back if the browser paused the tab.

Can I use it on a TV or projector?

Yes. Tap Full screen.
