Resize Image

Change the size of your photos in pixels or by percentage, or tap a social media size and the photo is cropped to fit.

🔒 Private: everything happens on your device. Nothing you type or upload is sent anywhere.

How to use it

  1. Tap a size (for example Instagram post 1080×1080) or type the width and height you need.
  2. If you type only the width or only the height, the other one is calculated to keep the proportions.
  3. Choose or drop your photos and download the resized copies.

Everything happens in your browser: your photos are never uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

What does 'Crop to fill' do?

It fills the whole size without stretching, cutting the edges that do not fit. 'Fit inside' keeps the whole photo and adds margins.

Can I make a photo bigger?

Yes, up to 400%, but enlarging cannot add detail, so very large enlargements look soft.

Can I resize many photos at once?

Yes, up to 30 at a time with the same settings.
