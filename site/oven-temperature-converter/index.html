The formula

°C = (°F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9 and °F = °C × 9 ÷ 5 + 32. So 350 °F is 177 °C, which most recipes round to 175 or 180 °C.

Fan (convection) ovens

A fan oven cooks faster, so lower the temperature by about 20 °C (25 °F) or check for doneness earlier.

Frequently asked questions

What is 350°F in Celsius?

About 177 °C. Most recipes use 180 °C, or 160 °C in a fan oven.

What is 200°C in Fahrenheit?

392 °F, usually rounded to 400 °F.

Do I lower the temperature for a convection oven?

Yes, by about 25 °F (20 °C), or keep the temperature and shorten the time.
