How to calculate square feet

Square feet = length × width (both in feet). A 12 × 10 ft room is 120 sq ft. If you measured in inches, the calculator divides by 144; in meters it multiplies by 10.764.

L-shaped and odd rooms

Split the room into rectangles, calculate each one and add them up. For a closet or a hallway that has the same size as another area, use the "number of equal areas" field.

How much extra to buy

  • Carpet, vinyl plank, laminate: 5% to 10% extra.
  • Tile: 10%, or 15% for diagonal patterns.
  • Hardwood: 7% to 10%.

Leftover pieces are useful for repairs later, and boxes from a different batch can have a slightly different color.

Paint

For walls, multiply the perimeter of the room by the wall height. One gallon of paint covers about 350 to 400 sq ft per coat.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet is a 12x12 room?

144 square feet.

How do I convert square meters to square feet?

Multiply by 10.764. For example, 20 m² is about 215 sq ft.

How much does flooring cost per square foot?

It varies a lot by material and region. Enter the price from your store to see the total material cost; installation is extra.
