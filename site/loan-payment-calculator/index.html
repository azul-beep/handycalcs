The loan payment formula

Payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)−n), where P is the loan amount, r the monthly rate (APR ÷ 12) and n the number of months. A $20,000 loan at 7.5% for 5 years costs $400.76 a month and $4,046 in interest.

Shorter term or lower payment?

A longer term lowers the monthly payment but raises the total interest. The same $20,000 at 7.5% over 7 years is $307 a month, but the interest grows to about $5,770. If the budget allows it, the shorter term saves money.

Before you sign

  • Compare the APR, not just the payment.
  • Ask about prepayment penalties.
  • Check that the payment fits your family budget with room to spare.

Frequently asked questions

Does this work for mortgages?

For principal and interest only. A mortgage payment also includes taxes, insurance and sometimes PMI.

What is APR?

The annual percentage rate: the yearly cost of the loan including some fees.

Can I pay a loan off early?

Usually yes, and it saves interest. Check your contract for prepayment penalties.
