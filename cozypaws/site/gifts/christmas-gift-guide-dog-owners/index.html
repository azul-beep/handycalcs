Questions dog people ask

What is a good Christmas gift for a dog owner?

Pick something tied to their dog: a mug or ornament with their breed, a sweatshirt that says dog mom or dog dad, or a gift for the dog itself like a holiday bandana or a pet blanket. Personal beats expensive.

What do you get a dog mom who has everything?

A dated keepsake ornament, a cozy plush blanket or a funny sweatshirt. They are things she would not buy for herself, and the ornament comes back every December.

What is a good gift for someone with a new puppy?

A Puppy's First Christmas ornament with the year on it, plus something practical for the puppy like a bowl or a warm hoodie.

What can I give a friend whose dog passed away?

A memorial ornament is a gentle, lasting gift. Add a short handwritten note with a memory of their dog.

When should I order to get it before Christmas?

Physical gifts are printed on demand in the USA: order by December 10 for Christmas delivery in the US. Printables download instantly, so they work up to Christmas Eve.
