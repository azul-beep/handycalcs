Paw Diary: a free pet diary and reminder app

Paw Diary keeps your dog or cat's daily life and health in one place. Log walks, meals, meds and moods with one tap, set reminders for vaccines, flea and tick pills, heartworm, grooming and vet visits, track weight with a chart and get a daily food plan. It is free, works in your browser and offline, and installs on your phone like a normal app.

What you can do

  • One tap daily log. Walk, meal, meds, potty, play, treat, grooming and vet buttons, with a walk counter for the day and the week.
  • Pet diary. Notes with date, time, mood, tags, weight and a photo, grouped by day.
  • Vet and care reminders. Vaccines, flea and tick, heartworm, deworming, grooming, nail trims, baths and checkups, repeating weekly, monthly, every 3 or 6 months or yearly. Add them to your phone calendar in one tap.
  • Weight tracker. A weight chart plus the healthy weight range for 70 dog breeds and 10 cat breeds.
  • Food calculator. Daily calories with the RER formula vets use, in cups or grams for your food, plus water and treat limits.
  • Private backup. Everything stays on your device. Download a backup file and restore it on a new phone.

Questions

Is Paw Diary free?

Yes, completely free, with no sign up and no in-app purchases.

Will it remind me when the app is closed?

The app shows what is due every time you open it, and can show an alert. To get a phone alert even when the app is closed, tap the calendar button and the reminder goes to your phone's calendar with an alarm.

Where is my data saved?

Only on your device, in your browser. Nothing is sent to us. Use "Download a backup" from time to time, especially before changing phones.

How do I install it on my phone?

On Android, tap "Install app" at the top (Chrome). On iPhone, open the page in Safari, tap Share and then "Add to Home Screen".

Is the food calculator veterinary advice?

No. It is a starting point based on the standard RER formula. Ask your vet before changing your pet's diet.
