Bulldog: Age in Human Years, Healthy Weight and How Much to Feed

Everything in one place for Bulldog owners: how old your dog is in human years, the healthy weight range and how much to feed every day. For your own dog's numbers, use our free Paw Diary app.

Healthy weight

A healthy adult Bulldog usually weighs 40 to 51 lb (18 to 23 kg). That makes it a medium breed, so after age 2 each year counts as about 5 human years.

Breed ranges are a guide. The best check is the body: you should feel the ribs with light pressure, see a waist from above and a tummy tuck from the side. Weigh every 2 to 4 weeks and track it in Paw Diary, which shows a chart and tells you if the weight is in the healthy range for the breed.

Questions

How much should a Bulldog weigh?

A healthy adult Bulldog usually weighs 40 to 51 lb (18 to 23 kg). Males are often at the top of the range and females at the bottom. Check the body too: you should feel the ribs easily and see a waist from above.

How old is a 5 year old Bulldog in human years?

About 39 human years. A 10 year old Bulldog is about 64.

How many calories does a Bulldog need a day?

A typical spayed or neutered adult Bulldog needs about 1079 calories a day (979 to 1176 depending on size). That is roughly 2.9 cups of a food with 370 kcal per cup. Ask your vet for your dog's exact needs.
